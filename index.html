About RSSDI-JHCON 2027

6th Annual Conference of the Research Society for the Study of Diabetes in India — Jharkhand Chapter

Dedicated to Diabetology Excellence

RSSDI-JHCON 2027 represents the 6th Annual Conference organised by the RSSDI Jharkhand Chapter (Research Society for the Study of Diabetes in India).

Scheduled across 8th, 9th, and 10th January 2027 at The Cruze, Adityapur, Jamshedpur, this clinical gathering unites physicians, diabetologists, and endocrine researchers across Jharkhand to exchange clinical updates, regional data, and patient management protocols.

Pioneering Heritage

Jamshedpur Gallery

Featured landmarks of Jamshedpur published directly on the official conference brochure

✨ Click any photo to open high-resolution lightbox
Amusement Park

Jubilee Amusement Park

Located inside Jubilee Park, offering family-friendly rides, vibrant landscaped gardens, and entertainment.

City of Steel

Tata Steel

The historic steel manufacturing plant founded by Jamsetji Tata in 1907, the industrial heartbeat of Jamshedpur.

Zoological Park

Tata Steel Zoological Park

A scenic wildlife sanctuary near Jubilee Park featuring diverse fauna, safari enclosures, and conservation programs.

Nature & Safari

Tata Steel Zoological Park

Expansive nature trails and peaceful watersides adjoining the zoo, ideal for nature lovers and birdwatching.

Spiritual Heritage

Sai Temple

A peaceful and revered temple dedicated to Sri Shirdi Sai Baba, situated in the tranquil Circuit House area.

Water & Splash Zone

Nicco Jubilee park and Splash Zone

A popular recreational attraction with wave pools, thrilling water slides, and fun entertainment for families.

Heritage & Culture

Russi Mody Center for Excellence

An architectural landmark preserving the archives, legacy, and industrial art collections of Tata Steel and Jamshedpur.

Wildlife Sanctuary

Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary

Sprawling deciduous forest across the Dalma hills, famous for its native elephant habitat and breathtaking viewpoints.
